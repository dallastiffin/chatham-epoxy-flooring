Getting A Price On Your Floor

The fastest way to get a real number is a photograph and a rough size.

A picture of the concrete tells us most of what we need: how old the slab is, whether it has been coated before, whether there is cracking or spalling, and how much preparation it is going to need. Descriptions rarely do.

From there we will either quote directly or come and measure, grind a test patch and take a moisture reading. Either way the visit is free and the quote is written.

Contact details and what to expect

What To Tell Us

Where the floor is and roughly how many square feet.

What happens on it. Parking, working, washing, storage, production.

What is on it now. Bare concrete, an old coating, tile adhesive, paint.

Whether the building is heated, which decides which resins are available in winter.

When you need it finished, and whether there is a window it has to fit into.
